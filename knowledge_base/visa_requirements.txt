BARBADOS VISA REQUIREMENTS BY NATIONALITY
Source: Consular Section, Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Foreign Trade, Barbados
Revised: May 09, 2025

This document lists the entry visa requirements for foreign nationals wishing to visit Barbados. It specifies whether a visa is required, and the permitted length of stay for each nationality.

---

COUNTRIES REQUIRING A VISA TO ENTER BARBADOS

The following nationals MUST obtain a visa before travelling to Barbados:
- Afghanistan
- Bosnia Herzegovina
- Dominican Republic
- Eritrea
- Haiti (holders of regular/ordinary passports only)
- Iran
- Iraq
- Kosovo
- Laos (Lao People's Democratic Republic)
- Libya
- Myanmar (Burma)
- North Korea (Democratic People's Republic of Korea)
- Papua New Guinea
- Somalia
- Syria
- Taiwan
- Yemen

Nationals of these countries must apply for a visa at the nearest Barbados Embassy, High Commission, or through a Barbados Honorary Consul before travel.

IMPORTANT NOTES ON ENTRY TO BARBADOS

1. Visa requirements may change. Always verify current requirements with the Barbados Ministry of Foreign Affairs or the nearest Barbados diplomatic mission before travelling.

2. Holding a valid visa or being visa-exempt does not guarantee entry. Immigration officers may refuse entry if a traveller cannot satisfy requirements.

3. All visitors must have:
   - A valid passport (valid for at least 6 months beyond intended stay for most nationalities)
   - A return or onward ticket
   - Sufficient funds for the duration of stay
   - Accommodation details (hotel booking or host address in Barbados)

4. Citizens of countries not listed above should contact the Barbados Ministry of Foreign Affairs or nearest Barbados Embassy/High Commission to determine their visa status.

5. Diplomatic and official passport holders may have different requirements from ordinary passport holders (e.g., Haiti — diplomatic/official passport holders are visa-exempt, but regular passport holders require a visa).

6. CARICOM nationals travelling on a CARICOM Skills Certificate (for skilled nationals) are admitted as nationals under CARICOM free movement provisions.
